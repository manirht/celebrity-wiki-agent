# Tom Hanks

## Introduction
Thomas Jeffrey Hanks, known as Tom Hanks, is an American actor and filmmaker. He was born on July 9, 1956, and has become one of the most celebrated and recognizable actors in the world. Hanks is known for his versatile acting, winning numerous awards, including two Academy Awards.

## Key Achievements
- Tom Hanks has won two Academy Awards for Best Actor, for his roles in 'Philadelphia' (1993) and 'Forrest Gump' (1994).
  - Sources: [src5](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000158/bio/)
- He has also received numerous other accolades, including Golden Globe Awards, Screen Actors Guild Awards, and Emmy Awards.
  - Sources: [src2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hanks)
- Hanks is known for his roles in iconic films such as 'Cast Away', 'The Green Mile', and 'Apollo 13'.

## Birthplace
Tom Hanks was born in Concord, California, USA. He spent his childhood in the greater San Francisco Bay Area, where he lived and worked before pursuing his acting career.
